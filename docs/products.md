# Products

Our products are built on simplicity and designed for everyday use.

---

## ReplyBridge

- **Features:** Instagram comment & DM automation (Auto DM, Auto Reply), per-post automation window, keywords, once-per-user rules, analytics.
- **Audience:** Operators of Instagram business and creator accounts.
- **Learn more:** [Get started (install & setup)](/replybridge/getting-started) — main link for Instagram DMs; [Overview](/replybridge/) — [Marketing & tips](/replybridge/marketing/)

## EmotionCare

- **Features:** Emotion input, personalized messages, push notifications, history-based personalization.
- **Business model:** In-app purchase (ticket-based).
- **Tech:** Cloud Run, Datastore, Vertex AI (Gemini).
- **Link:** https://apps.apple.com/app/emotioncare/id6483000351

## PackCheck

- **Features:** Photo-based luggage weight estimation, manual add/edit items, live totals.
- **Business model:** Ads (AdMob interstitials).
- **Tech:** Cloud Run, Vertex AI (multimodal).
- **Link:** https://apps.apple.com/app/packcheck/id6744557596

## HandWarmer
