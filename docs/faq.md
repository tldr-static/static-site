# Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

## Q. How do paid purchases and refunds work?

In-app purchases follow App Store policies. For issues, you can request a refund through the store.

## Q. Why do I see ads?

Some free apps are funded by ad revenue. We design frequency and placement carefully to avoid intrusive experiences.

## Q. How is my personal data handled?

We collect only what we need to provide the service and delete it when no longer required. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) for details.

## Q. Do you work with partners or collaborators?

Yes. Share your goals and scope on the [Contact](/contact) page and we will review and respond.

## Q. What is the support email?

main@tl-dr.in. We aim to reply as soon as possible.
