# Connect your Instagram

You only need to do this **once** (or again if Instagram asks you to reconnect). You **do not** need to be a developer or build your own Instagram app—ReplyBridge handles the technical side.

**New to ReplyBridge?** Start with **[Get started — install & set up](/replybridge/getting-started)** first.

---

## What you need

| Requirement | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Business or Creator account** | Instagram only allows this kind of automation for these account types. |
| **Facebook Page link (when required)** | Instagram often connects through a Facebook Page. The ReplyBridge app will tell you if something is missing. |

---

## What happens when you tap “Connect”

1. You stay inside **ReplyBridge** until it opens a **browser window** (usually Safari).
2. You **log in** to Facebook/Instagram and **allow** the permissions the app asks for. These let ReplyBridge read comments on your posts and send the messages **you** configure.
3. You return to ReplyBridge and should see **Connected** or similar.

If any step fails, check your internet, update the Instagram and Facebook apps, and try **Connect** again.

---

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| “Can’t connect” or login loops | Close the browser tab, reopen ReplyBridge, tap Connect again. |
| Permissions denied | In phone settings, allow the browser to complete login; then retry in the app. |
| Instagram disconnected | Open the Instagram app and confirm your account and Page are still linked; reconnect in ReplyBridge. |
| Token expired | Use **Reconnect** or **Connect again** inside ReplyBridge. |

Still stuck? Use [Contact](/contact) with your Instagram handle and a screenshot if possible.
