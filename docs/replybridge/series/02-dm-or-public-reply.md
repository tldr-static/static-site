# Episode 2 — Private DM vs public reply

ReplyBridge can do **two different things** on the same post:

| | **Auto DM (private)** | **Public reply (under the comment)** |
|---|------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Who sees it** | Only the person who commented | Everyone reading the thread |
| **Best for** | Links, coupons, “DM us this word,” personal next steps | Short FAQs: hours, shipping policy, “yes, we ship” |

---

## Simple rule of thumb

- Put **sensitive or long** stuff in the **DM** (price lists, long links, codes).
- Put **one-line answers** that help **everyone** in the **public reply**.

You can use **both** on one post, or only one—your choice per post.
