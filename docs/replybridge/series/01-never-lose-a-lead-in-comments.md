# Episode 1 — Never lose a lead in the comments

Someone comments **“How much?”** or **“Link?”** on your post. If you answer late—or miss it—they may already be gone.

**ReplyBridge** helps you respond **right away**: a **private DM** with your price, link, or next step, and optionally a **short public reply** so others see the answer too.

---

## What you should remember

1. **Speed feels like care** — Fast first replies build trust.
2. **Same question, many people** — Automation saves you from copy-paste burnout.
3. **You stay in control** — You write the message; the app sends it when **your** rules match.
