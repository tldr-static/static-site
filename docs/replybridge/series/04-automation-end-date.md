# Episode 4 — Your automation has an end date (on purpose)

Sales and launches **end**. Your automation should too—otherwise old posts keep promising deals you no longer run.

ReplyBridge lets you set a **shared end time** for Auto DM and public reply on a post. When time’s up, automation **stops** and you get a clear signal to check the app.

---

## Why creators like this

- No awkward “sorry, that promo ended” DMs weeks later.  
- **Event posts** and **seasonal drops** stay tidy.  
- You can **extend** in the app when it makes sense (where the app offers that option).
