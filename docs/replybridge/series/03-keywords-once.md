# Episode 3 — Keywords & “once per person”

Two features keep automation **clean**:

## Keywords

Only send your DM when the comment includes words you care about—e.g. **INFO**, **PRICE**, **LINK**.

**Why it helps:** Random emoji-only comments or spam don’t trigger your message.

## Once per person

On a single post, each user gets your DM **only once** (when you turn this on).

**Why it helps:** Great for **giveaways**, **lead magnets**, or events where you don’t want the same person spamming comments for multiple DMs.

---

## Try this on your next launch

1. Pick **one keyword** you say in the caption (easy to copy).  
2. Turn on **once per person** if it’s a promo or contest.  
3. Watch your stats in the app to see how many people matched.
