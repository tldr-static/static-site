# Episode 6 — Why “official” Instagram tools matter

ReplyBridge is built for **Instagram Business and Creator** accounts and uses **supported** ways to read comments and send messages—so you’re not relying on shady “gray area” bots that can put your account at risk.

---

## What you’ll do in the app

- **Connect** Instagram through the normal login and permission screens.  
- **No developer console** and no copying secret keys—you’re a business owner, not an engineer.

If connection fails, it’s usually fixable: update the Instagram app, confirm your **Business/Creator** setup, and try **Connect** again. More help: **[Connect Instagram](/replybridge/integration)**
