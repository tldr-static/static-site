# How to use ReplyBridge

After **[installing and connecting Instagram](/replybridge/getting-started)**, you manage everything **inside the app**: pick posts, write messages, and choose when automation should run.

---

## The usual flow

1. Open your **list of posts** in ReplyBridge.
2. **Choose one post** to start (you can add more later).
3. Turn on automation and fill in:
   - **DM text** — what people receive in private messages.
   - **Optional public reply** — what everyone sees under the comment.
   - **Optional keywords** — only answer comments that contain certain words.
4. Set **how long** automation stays on (great for sales or events).
5. Save. New comments on that post can trigger your messages **automatically**.

---

## Auto DM vs public reply

| | Best for… |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto DM** | Links, coupons, “DM us this word”, personal next steps—**private**. |
| **Public reply** | Short FAQs everyone should see—**visible on the post**. |

You can use **both**, **one**, or switch them per post.

---

## Keywords & “once per person”

- **Keywords** — Example: only send a DM if the comment includes “info” or “price”. Reduces noise and spammy replies.
- **Once per person** — Sends your DM only **once** to the same person on that post—useful for giveaways or collecting leads fairly.

---

## Automation “until when?”

Your DM and public reply can share **one end date**. When the time is up, automation stops—so you don’t accidentally keep promoting an old sale.

---

## Quick checks

- Wrong post selected? Open that post in ReplyBridge and adjust settings.
- No DM arrived? Check keywords, time limit, and that Instagram is still connected.
- Too many DMs? Tighten keywords or turn on “once per person.”
