# Practical tips

Short **marketing and operations** snippets you can reuse. When adding tips, group them by date or number for easier maintenance.

---

## Message frames

### Problem → Agitate → Solve

1. “Still replying to every Instagram comment by hand?”
2. “Missed replies cost revenue; answering all costs your day.”
3. “ReplyBridge sends **DMs and public replies** automatically—on your rules.”

### Before → After

- **Before:** 100 comments, replies at midnight.
- **After:** 100 comments—DMs and public replies fire per your rules.

---

## Caption & Reel hooks (examples)

- “What if a DM went out the moment someone commented—on Meta’s official path?”
- “Answer the same question once in-thread with an official public reply.”
- “When the event ends, automation ends too—match the window to the post.”

---

## Stories & CTAs

- Use story question stickers: “What’s hardest about auto-replies right now?”
- Use link stickers and profile links with UTMs per campaign (e.g. `utm_campaign=auto-dm-basics`).
