# Content strategy overview

A concise plan to turn ReplyBridge’s official channels (especially Instagram) into **saves, profile visits, and installs**. For full copy and PNG specs, see reply-bridge `marketing/STRATEGY.md`.

---

## Positioning

> An app that turns Instagram comment and DM responses into a **clean, automated official path**.

- **Audience:** Teams that run Instagram **business or creator** accounts **solo or with a small crew**.
- **Differentiation:** Meta **official APIs** (Private Reply, Comments API), **per-post automation window**, **keywords** and **once-per-user** rules to reduce spam.
- **Tone:** Editorial, spacious typography—*Digital Curator*—not hype or emoji overload.

---

## Audience segments (summary)

| Segment | Core message |
| --- | --- |
| Solo creators (~5k–100k) | “From comment to DM—automatically.” |
| One-person ecommerce / SMB | “Keywords + once per user—no spam.” |
| Agency / brand manager | “Per-post sends and reach you can report.” |

Start with creators and SMB; expand report-style content later.

---

## Content pillars (mix guide)

| Code | Name | Role | Mix (guide) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **F1** | Teach | Explain Private Reply, keywords, windows | ~30% |
| **F2** | Use case | Launches, events, real scenarios | ~25% |
| **F3** | Product | Feature highlights, UI tips | ~20% |
| **F4** | Proof | Testimonials, stats screenshots, quotes | ~15% |
| **F5** | Community | Q&A, lessons learned, roadmap polls | ~10% |

---

## Format mix (examples)

| Format | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Vertical multi-slide feed posts | Saves & shares |
| Short Reels (screen capture, before/after) | Reach |
| Square single-image posts | Product shots & reviews |
| Stories | Q&A, links, cross-promotion |

Post when your audience is likely scrolling (e.g. weekday mornings/evenings) and refine with data.
