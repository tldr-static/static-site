# ReplyBridge

**ReplyBridge** helps you handle Instagram **comments and DMs** faster. When someone comments on your post, it can automatically send them a **private DM** you wrote in advance and/or leave a **public reply** under their comment—so you spend less time typing the same answers.

This site is written for **creators, shop owners, and social marketers** who use Instagram every day. You don’t need a technical background.

## What you can do

| Feature | In everyday words |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto DM** | Send a private message to people who comment—great for links, prices, or next steps. |
| **Auto reply** | Post a public answer under the comment so everyone sees it (like a mini FAQ). |
| **Keywords** | Only react when the comment includes words you care about (e.g. “sale”, “info”). |
| **Once per person** | Avoid sending the same DM over and over to the same user on one post. |
| **Time limit** | Turn automation off automatically after a date—handy for events or limited offers. |
| **Simple stats** | See how many messages went out and how many people commented—so you know what works. |

---

## Is it for me?

ReplyBridge fits if you:

- Run a **Business** or **Creator** Instagram and get lots of similar questions in comments or DMs.
- Want to **save time** without ignoring your audience.
- Prefer clear, **Instagram-approved** behavior instead of risky unofficial bots.
